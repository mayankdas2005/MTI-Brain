# Treasury Risk Watchlist — May 2026

## Watchlist summary

Treasury Risk has added the Week 7 / Week 8 liquidity trough to the May watchlist. The item is not a formal Red breach. It is an Amber liquidity item with an additional CFO / Board-commitment overlay.

## Watchlist item: Liquidity buffer compression

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Watchlist ID | TRW-2026-05-LIQ-01 |
| Risk category | Liquidity risk |
| Current status | Amber with Board-commitment overlay |
| Current total liquidity | USD 314.6M |
| Forecast low point | USD 231.4M |
| Forecast low point week | Week ending 2026-07-03 |
| Formal hard minimum | USD 200M |
| Target operating buffer | USD 300M |
| Active management commitment | Maintain at least USD 250M total liquidity through current planning window |
| Primary owner | Jonathan Reed |
| CFO briefing required | Yes |

## Risk interpretation

The forecast remains above the hard minimum liquidity threshold. On formal policy alone, the item should be treated as Amber because forecast liquidity falls below the target operating buffer and below the Green posture.

The issue is more material for the CFO briefing because the forecast low point is below the management commitment communicated to the Audit & Risk Committee. A standard threshold answer would not be sufficient because it would understate the Board-readiness dimension.

## Key drivers

1. Higher seasonal inventory receipts than prior-year corresponding period.
2. Supplier prepayments approved for selected private-label product lines.
3. Payroll and tax outflows concentrated in Weeks 4–6.
4. Recovery dependent on customer receipts clearing as scheduled in Week 8 / Week 9 transition.
5. No current breach of committed facility availability or debt covenant limits.

## Required response posture

Treasury should provide the CFO with a short recommendation before the briefing. The recommendation should include:

- whether to take an operational action to keep the trough above USD 250M;
- whether to pre-position facility capacity;
- whether to adjust payment timing;
- whether to accelerate collections;
- whether the CFO should brief the Audit & Risk Committee Chair before the formal pack is circulated.

## Mitigation options under review

| Option | Estimated impact | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Defer supplier acceleration by one week | +USD 8M at trough | Requires Sourcing approval |
| Accelerate top customer receipts | +USD 6M–12M at trough | Depends on commercial teams |
| Draw USD 25M revolver for 30 days | Keeps trough above USD 250M | Financing cost and optics must be considered |
| Combine supplier deferral and collection acceleration | +USD 14M–20M at trough | Likely sufficient without revolver draw if receipts land on time |

## Watchlist recommendation

The watchlist owner recommends that the CFO briefing classify the item as:

> Amber under formal policy; action required before CFO briefing because forecast liquidity is below the CFO / Audit & Risk Committee management commitment.

This distinction should remain visible in all CFO-facing materials.
