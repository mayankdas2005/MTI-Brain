## Seasonality assumptions

The forecast uses historical inflows and outflows from the corresponding prior-year period, adjusted for the following:

1. **Inventory seasonality.** The prior-year period included a lower inventory build. This year's plan assumes a 6.5% higher summer inventory receipt profile.
2. **Promotional cadence.** Summer campaign purchasing starts one week earlier than prior year in North America and Europe.
3. **Tax timing.** Indirect tax and import duty payment timing is consistent with last year but overlaps with supplier prepayments this year.
4. **Receivables normalization.** The forecast assumes two large customer receipts clear during Week 8 / Week 9 transition. A one-week slip would keep liquidity below USD 250M for an additional week.
5. **FX translation.** FX conversion uses treasury-approved rates as of 2026-05-12. Sensitivity to FX rates is not the primary driver of the forecast trough.

## Working interpretation

Under formal policy, the forecast does not breach the USD 200M hard minimum. However, the forecast spends several weeks below the target operating buffer and several weeks below the CFO's current management comfort level discussed with the Audit & Risk Committee. Treasury should not present the forecast as routine.

## Recommendation for CFO briefing preparation

Prepare two views:

1. **Formal policy view.** No Red breach. Amber monitoring required because forecast liquidity falls below target operating buffer and below the formal Green posture.
2. **Leadership commitment view.** Forecast low point of approximately USD 231M is below the CFO's USD 250M liquidity commitment, creating a Board-readiness issue unless mitigation or explanation is prepared.
