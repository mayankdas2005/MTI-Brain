   Current answer: not without mitigation or explanation. The projected trough falls below USD 250M.

## CFO direction

Maya instructed that the forecast should not be summarized as "policy compliant" without also explaining the Board-readiness issue. She wants a recommendation before the CFO briefing, not only a threshold calculation.

Direction recorded:

> If the forecast trough is still below USD 250M, bring me the mitigation options and the language we would use with the Committee. Do not make it sound like a policy breach, but also do not let it disappear as routine Amber monitoring.

The briefing pack should include:

- Current total liquidity.
- 4-week and 13-week liquidity forecast.
- Policy status: Green / Amber / Red.
- Forecast low point and driver explanation.
- Explicit note on the active CFO / Audit & Risk Committee liquidity commitment.
- Recommended action before the CFO briefing.
- Owner and timeline for each action.

## Closure criteria

This register item can be closed only when the CFO has reviewed the Week 8 liquidity trough, selected a mitigation or messaging option, and confirmed whether the USD 250M management commitment remains active for the next Audit & Risk Committee materials.
