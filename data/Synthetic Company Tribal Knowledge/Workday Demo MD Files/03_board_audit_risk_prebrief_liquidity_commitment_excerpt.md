# Audit & Risk Committee Prebrief — Liquidity Commitment Excerpt

## Context

This excerpt was captured from the CFO staff prebrief ahead of the March Audit & Risk Committee update. The discussion focused on liquidity resilience during the Q2–Q3 working-capital cycle and on whether management should communicate any change in the Group's risk appetite.

## Relevant discussion excerpt

Caroline asked whether the Board should expect the Group to operate close to the USD 200M hard minimum liquidity threshold during the Q2–Q3 planning period.

Maya responded that management did not intend to run the business near the hard floor. She stated that the USD 200M threshold remains the formal policy minimum, but it is not the level at which management wants the Committee to think about comfort.

Maya's wording as captured in the meeting record:

> We should not signal that USD 200M is our operating comfort level. The Group can be compliant at that number, but that is not how I want to manage the Board conversation. For this planning window, I am comfortable committing that we will maintain at least USD 250M of total liquidity. If the forecast shows us moving below that level, I want it surfaced before it reaches the Board pack.

Jonathan noted that this was a management commitment rather than a policy amendment. Maya agreed and said the formal policy should not be changed outside the scheduled annual review unless the Committee explicitly requests it.

## Clarification from Corporate Secretary

Michael confirmed after the prebrief that the above statement should be treated as an active CFO commitment for Board-readiness purposes. It should not be inserted into the formal Group Treasury Policy without Audit & Risk Committee recommendation and Board approval.

## Practical handling agreed

- Formal policy remains unchanged: USD 200M hard minimum liquidity threshold.
- The target operating buffer remains USD 300M.
- The CFO commitment for the current planning window is to maintain at least USD 250M of total liquidity.
- A forecast below USD 250M should be surfaced to the CFO before inclusion in any Board or Audit & Risk Committee materials.
- Treasury should distinguish between formal compliance and Board-commitment risk.

## Current status

The commitment was not retired, superseded, or amended in subsequent March or April records. It remains active until confirmed otherwise by the CFO or Corporate Secretary.
