# Teams Transcript Excerpt — CFO Briefing Prep

## Transcript context

Meeting purpose: prepare CFO talking points for the May treasury health briefing and confirm how to handle the 13-week liquidity forecast.

Transcript excerpt begins at 00:18:42.

## Excerpt

**Jonathan Reed:** Current liquidity is still above USD 300M. The 13-week forecast is where we need the discussion. The low point is now approximately USD 231M in the July trough. It never gets close to the USD 200M hard floor.

**Maya Patel:** That is not enough for the briefing. The question is not just whether we breach the hard floor. We told the Committee we would manage this period with at least USD 250M of liquidity.

**Priya Shah:** Do you want that described as a policy threshold or as a management commitment?

**Maya Patel:** Management commitment. Do not rewrite the policy. The formal policy has not changed. But if our internal forecast shows us below USD 250M, I need to see that before the CFO briefing and before anything goes into the Board materials.

**Elena Morris:** Should the recommendation be to draw on the revolver to stay above USD 250M, or just explain the trough?

**Maya Patel:** Bring options. I do not want the first recommendation to be an automatic draw. I want to know what it costs, what else we can move, and whether a one-week receipt delay creates a bigger issue.

**Lina Ortiz:** From a policy standpoint, this is Amber. It requires action before the CFO briefing but it is not a Red breach.

**Maya Patel:** Correct. Keep that distinction. Amber under policy. Board-commitment risk under the management commitment. Both need to be visible.

**Jonathan Reed:** We will separate the briefing into formal policy status, forecast drivers, and management commitment impact.

**Maya Patel:** Good. The final note should say whether action is required before the CFO briefing. My view is yes if the low point remains below USD 250M.

## Practical interpretation

The CFO's direction is to treat the forecast low point below USD 250M as requiring action before the CFO briefing, even though the forecast remains above the formal hard minimum threshold of USD 200M.

The recommended response should not describe the USD 250M level as a formal policy limit. It should describe it as an active CFO / Audit & Risk Committee management commitment that changes the briefing recommendation.
