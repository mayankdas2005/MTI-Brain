# CFO Monthly Finance / Treasury Review — April 2026

## Meeting objective

Review current Group liquidity, May–July cash forecast posture, debt and covenant status, FX exposure, interest-rate sensitivity, and items requiring CFO attention before the next treasury health briefing.

## Current liquidity position

Treasury reported total liquidity of **USD 318.4M** as of close of business 28 April 2026. This includes unrestricted cash, cash equivalents, and committed undrawn facilities available within five business days, net of trapped and restricted balances.

| Liquidity component | USD millions | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Unrestricted cash and cash equivalents | 188.6 | Concentrated in North America and UK treasury pools |
| Available committed revolving credit facility | 160.0 | Available within five business days |
| Less restricted / trapped cash | (30.2) | Primarily LATAM and local regulatory balances |
| **Total liquidity** | **318.4** | Above target operating buffer at month end |

## CFO comments

Maya asked Treasury to avoid presenting the current liquidity position as purely Green if the forecast shows material compression later in the planning window. Her concern is that the CFO briefing has historically over-weighted current position and under-weighted forecast timing.

Direct note from the CFO:

> The current number is fine, but the briefing needs to show whether the next 13 weeks create a decision point. I do not want the discussion to stop at today's balance if we already know the trough is coming in July.

## Items Treasury leadership is watching

1. **Week 6–8 liquidity compression.** Treasury expects a seasonal working-capital drawdown from inventory receipts, payroll timing, and tax-related outflows.
2. **Receivables timing.** Two large customer receipts are forecast for Week 9 but have normal timing variability of five to eight business days.
3. **Supplier payment acceleration.** Sourcing has requested early payments for selected private-label suppliers ahead of the summer promotional cycle.
4. **FX settlement timing.** Hedge settlements in EUR and JPY are not individually material, but the timing overlaps with the forecast trough.
5. **CFO briefing posture.** Treasury should prepare a clear recommendation, not only a dashboard readout.

## Formal policy posture

Jonathan confirmed that the formal hard minimum liquidity threshold remains **USD 200M** and that the target operating buffer is **USD 300M**. Operating below the target buffer but above the hard floor is permitted, but the CFO expects the upcoming briefing to include actions, owners, and timing for any Amber condition.

## Informal leadership context

Maya reminded the group that she gave the Audit & Risk Committee comfort that management would maintain a liquidity cushion materially above the hard floor through the current quarter. She asked Jonathan and Priya to verify whether the current forecast remains consistent with that leadership expectation before the next CFO briefing.

The CFO did not request a policy amendment during this meeting. The instruction was to treat the item as an active leadership commitment until the next policy review.

## Decisions

- Treasury will continue using total liquidity as the headline metric in the CFO briefing.
- The 13-week forecast will be shown alongside current liquidity.
- Any week below the target operating buffer must be explained with drivers and mitigation options.
- Treasury will provide a clear recommendation on whether action is required before the CFO briefing.
