# CFO Staff Briefing - AP Dispute Review and Vendor Payment-Term Sensitivities

**Date:** 17 April 2026
**Time:** 09:00-09:45 BST
**Location:** Microsoft Teams
**Meeting owner:** CFO Office
**Recorder:** CFO Staff Operations
**Classification:** Internal - CFO Staff / Corporate Development /
Treasury Leadership

## Attendees

- Chief Financial Officer
- Group Treasurer
- VP, Corporate Development
- Director, Procurement
- Director, Accounts Payable
- IT Finance Lead
- Legal Counsel, Corporate Transactions
- Head of Enterprise Applications

## Purpose

Review suppliers with open disputed invoices before any payment-term
renegotiation activity is initiated. Confirm whether any Net-30
suppliers with disputed invoices require exception handling because of
strategic, legal, procurement, or corporate-development considerations.

## Summary

Accounts Payable confirmed that several Net-30 suppliers currently have
invoices marked as **DISPUTE**. Treasury noted that extending disputed
Net-30 suppliers to Net-90 could create a working-capital benefit if
applied broadly. The CFO directed the team to separate normal commercial
renegotiation candidates from suppliers currently under strategic
review.

Two suppliers must be excluded from any Net-90 renegotiation activity at
this time:

1. **IT/SaaS Vendor 10**
2. **IT/SaaS Vendor 5**

Both suppliers are under active corporate-development review as
potential near-term acquisition or strategic investment targets. Their
invoices may remain in disputed status for operational reconciliation
reasons, but they should not be treated as leverage points for
payment-term extension.

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Disputed invoice population

The AP Director confirmed that invoices marked **DISPUTE** include
several categories:

- usage true-ups,
- service-credit reviews,
- contract-line reconciliation,
- purchase-order matching issues,
- timing differences between vendor billing and internal cost-center
  allocation.

The CFO asked AP and Procurement not to assume that every disputed
invoice represents supplier performance weakness or negotiation
leverage.

### 2. IT/SaaS Vendor 10

The VP, Corporate Development confirmed that **IT/SaaS Vendor 10 IT** (with third party code = 'TP_IT_0010')
is in active diligence as a potential acquisition target. The supplier
provides a platform component currently used by Enterprise Applications
and has been flagged as strategically relevant to the FY2026 digital
operating model.

The current invoice dispute relates to usage-tier reconciliation and
internal cost-center mapping. It is not a supplier-performance dispute.

The CFO directed that **IT/SaaS Vendor 10** must remain on current
Net-30 treatment** until Corporate Development completes the current
diligence cycle.

**Rationale:** Moving the supplier from Net-30 to Net-90 while diligence
is active could create avoidable relationship friction, alter the
commercial tone of the acquisition discussion, and signal financial
pressure inconsistent with the company's intended posture as a potential
buyer.

### 3. IT/SaaS Vendor 5

The Head of Enterprise Applications confirmed that **IT/SaaS Vendor 5** (with third party code = 'TP_IT_0005')
supports a workflow capability currently being evaluated for deeper
integration into the enterprise architecture. The VP, Corporate
Development confirmed that the supplier is also being reviewed as a
possible strategic investment or tuck-in acquisition candidate.

The current invoice dispute relates to license-count reconciliation and
a pending service-credit calculation. It is not a basis for payment-term
pressure.

The CFO directed that **IT/SaaS Vendor 5** must remain on current Net-30
treatment** until the Corporate Development and Legal teams clear the
next decision gate.

**Rationale:** Moving the supplier from Net-30 to Net-90 could weaken
trust during a sensitive strategic evaluation, complicate diligence
communications, and create unnecessary risk to a supplier relationship
that may become strategically important.

## CFO Direction

The CFO stated that the working-capital team may continue evaluating
Net-30 suppliers with disputed invoices for possible Net-90
renegotiation, but **IT/SaaS Vendor 10 IT** and **IT/SaaS Vendor 5** are
not to be included in the target population.

The CFO emphasized that disputed status alone should not drive
renegotiation decisions when a supplier is tied to active
corporate-development activity.

## Decision

**Exclude IT/SaaS Vendor 10 IT and IT/SaaS Vendor 5 from the Net-90
payment-term renegotiation list.**

These suppliers should remain on current Net-30 payment terms until
Corporate Development, Legal, and the CFO Office confirm that
acquisition or investment discussions are no longer active.

## Notes for CFO Briefing

The CFO may accept a Net-90 renegotiation strategy for disputed Net-30
suppliers only after excluding suppliers with active strategic
sensitivity.

The working-capital benefit should be reported in two views:

1. gross benefit before supplier exclusions;
2. adjusted benefit after excluding protected suppliers.

The adjusted view is the preferred version for CFO review.
