# Purpose

This policy establishes the principles, limits, controls, and decision rules governing all treasury activity across the Group. It defines how liquidity, funding, foreign exchange, interest rate, and counterparty exposures are measured, monitored, and controlled, and the level at which each must be managed.

This policy sets the quantitative thresholds and triggers that determine when a treasury position requires management action and escalation. These triggers are the authoritative reference for assessing, ahead of any CFO treasury briefing, whether the current position can be reported as routine or whether action is required.

# Scope

This policy applies to the Group and all majority-owned subsidiaries, and to every officer and employee who initiates, executes, records, or oversees a treasury transaction. It covers:

- liquidity and cash management, including cash forecasting.

- funding, debt, and capital structure, including covenant compliance.

- foreign exchange (FX) risk

- interest rate risk

- counterparty, credit and investment risk.

- treasury reporting, escalation, and governance.

# Treasury Objectives

Treasury is managed to the following objectives, applied strictly in order of priority. A lower-priority objective is never pursued at the expense of a higher one.

- **Capital preservation:** Protect the principal value of the Group's financial assets.

- **Liquidity sufficiency:** Ensure the Group can meet all obligations as they fall due, in all reasonably foreseeable conditions, without reliance on uncommitted funding.

- **Risk control:** Keep market and credit exposures within the limits approved by the Board.

- **Return optimisation:** Optimise net financing cost and yield on surplus funds, subordinate to the objectives above.

# Governance and Responsibilities

Treasury operates under delegated authority from the Board, with segregation between execution (front office), risk and compliance monitoring (middle office) and settlement and recording (back office).

- **Board of Directors:** Approves this policy and the Group's treasury risk appetite, including the minimum liquidity threshold and all hard limits.

- **Audit & Risk Committee:** Provides oversight, reviews all limit breaches and exceptions, and recommends policy changes to the Board.

- **Chief Financial Officer (CFO):** Accountable for execution of this policy, chairs the Treasury Committee, approves exceptions within delegated authority, and is the recipient of treasury escalations and the CFO treasury briefing.

- **Group Treasurer:** Responsible for day-to-day treasury management and maintaining the cash forecasts and risk measures. Monitors every position against the limits in this policy and classifies status against the Action Triggers. Prepares the CFO briefing and is the first point of escalation.

- **Treasury Committee:** Meets at least monthly to review the Group position against limits and to approve mitigating actions.

# Liquidity Risk Management

## Definition of Total Liquidity

*Total Liquidity* means unrestricted cash and cash equivalents, plus undrawn committed credit facilities available for drawing within five business days, less any cash that is restricted, trapped or otherwise not available to the Group within that period. Total Liquidity is the headline measure reported daily and in every CFO briefing.

## Minimum Liquidity Threshold

The Group shall always maintain Total Liquidity of no less than USD 200 million (Minimum Liquidity Threshold). The threshold applies both to the current position and to every week of the approved forecast horizon. It is a hard, Board-approved limit and any actual or projected breach is a red trigger under as per *Action Triggers and Escalation Framework*.

## Target Operating Buffer

In addition to the Minimum Liquidity Threshold, the Group targets a Total Liquidity operating buffer of at least USD 300 million to absorb forecasting error and timing volatility. Operating between USD 200M and USD 300M is permitted but constitutes an early-warning (Amber) condition requiring documented monitoring.

## Cash Forecasting

Treasury shall maintain and update the following forecasts, reconciling actuals to prior forecasts each cycle:

- A rolling 13-week direct cash forecast and is updated weekly.

- A 4-week short-term liquidity forecast for near-term operational planning and

- A 3-month medium-term forecast for funding and investment decisions.

Forecasts shall be built from historical inflows and outflows and seasonally adjusted using actuals from the corresponding period of the prior year. Every forecast must explicitly flag any week in which projected Total Liquidity falls below the Minimum Liquidity Threshold, and any week in which it falls below the Target Operating Buffer.

# Funding and Debt Management

The Group maintains diversified funding sources and a staggered maturity profile to avoid concentration of refinancing risk. The following limits apply:

- Net leverage (net debt / EBITDA) shall not exceed the maintenance covenant level of 3.50x

- Interest cover (EBITDA / net interest expense) shall not fall below 3.00x

- No individual debt maturity shall be left unfunded within 90 days of its maturity date.

- A documented refinancing plan shall exist for every maturity falling due within the next 12 months.

Treasury team monitors covenant headroom each month and projects covenant ratios over the next two quarters. Projected erosion of headroom is escalated under the section on *Action Triggers and Escalation Framework*.

# Foreign Exchange Risk Management

The Group hedges to reduce the impact of currency movements on committed cash flows and reported earnings. Speculative positions are prohibited.

- A highly probable transactional FX exposure within a rolling 12-month horizon shall be hedged between 80% and 100% of committed amount.

- Net open position in any single currency shall not exceed USD 25 million

- Aggregate net open position across all currencies shall not exceed USD 50 million.

# Interest Rate Risk Management

The Group manages the balance between fixed and floating rate debt to provide predictability of financing cost while retaining flexibility.

- Fixed-rate proportion of gross debt shall be maintained between 40% and 70%.

- The modelled impact of a +100-basis point parallel rate shift on annual net interest expense shall not exceed USD 10 million

- Interest rate hedging instruments shall be used only to manage existing or highly probable exposure.

# Counterparty and Investment Risk

Cash and investments are placed only with approved, creditworthy counterparties and in permitted instruments.

- Counterparties shall carry a minimum long-term credit rating of A- or AAA

- No more than 30% of total cash and investments shall be held with any single counterparty, other than approved sovereign or government money market instruments.

- Permitted instruments are limited to bank deposits, government securities, and prime money market funds rated AAА-equivalent.

# Action Triggers and Escalation Framework

This section is the authoritative basis for determining whether a treasury position requires action. Each monitored dimension is classified using a three-level status model:

- **Green:** Within risk appetite. No action required beyond routine reporting.

- **Amber:** Early warning. A metric is approaching a limit and action is required before the next CFO briefing.

- **Red:** A limit is breached, or a breach is imminent or projected. Immediate action and escalation are required.

**Decision rules**

- A treasury position requires action whenever any monitored metric is classified Amber or Red.

- Where every monitored metric is Green, the position requires no action beyond routine reporting and may be reported as such in the CFO briefing.

- Any Amber or Red status must be named in the briefing together with its required action, owner, and timeline.

# Reporting and the CFO Briefing

Treasury produces the following standard reporting:

- Daily liquidity report showing Total Liquidity against the Minimum Liquidity Threshold and Target Operating Buffer

- Weekly 13-week cash forecast with seasonality adjustment and threshold flags

- Monthly Treasury Committee pack covering all dimensions under the *Action Triggers and Escalation Framework* section.

- CFO treasury briefing.

The CFO treasury briefing is a one-page summary of treasury health covering liquidity, debt, FX, interest rate exposure, and key risks. It states, for each dimension, the current status under *Action Triggers and Escalation Framework* section and an overall conclusion as to whether the position requires action. The briefing is produced ahead of each scheduled CFO review and is triggered immediately on any red classification.

# Policy Exceptions and Breaches

Any breach of a hard limit, and any approved exception, shall be reported to the CFO within one business day and to the Audit & Risk Committee at its next meeting, accompanied by a documented remediation plan and timeline. Exceptions are valid only were approved in writing within delegated authority and for a defined period.

# Policy Review and Approval

This policy is reviewed at least annually by the Group Treasurer and CFO, and on any material change to the Group's funding structure, risk appetite, or operating environment. Material changes are recommended by the Audit & Risk Committee and approved by the Board.

# Appendix A - Key Definitions

- **Total Liquidity:** Unrestricted cash and cash equivalents plus undrawn committed facilities available within five business days, less restricted or trapped cash.

- **Minimum Liquidity Threshold:** The hard floor of USD 200M that Total Liquidity must not fall below on either an actual or forecast basis.

- **Target Operating Buffer:** The USD 300M Total Liquidity level the Group aims to operate above.

- **Net leverage:** Net debt divided by trailing-twelve-month EBITDA.

- **Hedge ratio:** The proportion of committed or highly probable FX exposure covered by hedging instruments over the relevant horizon.

- **Net open position:** The unhedged residual FX exposure in a currency or in aggregate.
