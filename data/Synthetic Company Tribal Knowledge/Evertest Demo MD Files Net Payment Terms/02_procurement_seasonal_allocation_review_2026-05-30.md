# Procurement Seasonal Allocation Review - Apparel and Logistics Suppliers

## Group and entity context

These notes relate to **GlobalRetail Holdings plc** (`GR_HOLDINGS`, LEI `LEI-GRHOLD`), the UK parent company of a 25-entity GlobalRetail group. The parent functional currency is **GBP**. Working-capital analysis, supplier-notice decisions, and CFO briefing values in this folder should be treated as group-level analysis for `GR_HOLDINGS` unless a source line identifies a specific regional or operating-company dependency.

**GlobalRetail Treasury Ltd** (`GR_TREASURY`, GBP) is the in-house treasury vehicle coordinating the Net-30 / Net-90 supplier payment-term workstream with the CFO Office, AP Operations, Procurement, Sourcing, and Store Development Finance. Regional context may involve **GlobalRetail Europe BV** (`GR_EU_BV`), **GlobalRetail APAC Pte** (`GR_APAC_PTE`), **GlobalRetail LATAM SA** (`GR_LATAM_SA`), and country operating companies such as GlobalRetail US Inc, GlobalRetail UK Ltd, GlobalRetail Deutschland GmbH, and GlobalRetail Japan KK.

## Entity scope noted during the review

Procurement confirmed that the seasonal allocation risks are not evenly distributed across the GlobalRetail group. The consolidated payment-term opportunity belongs to `GR_HOLDINGS` / `GR_TREASURY`, but individual supplier decisions may affect regional or country operating-company execution. This review focused on APAC apparel allocation and US logistics capacity.


**Date:** 2026-05-30  
**Meeting type:** Procurement / Sourcing seasonal readiness review  
**Attendees:** VP Procurement, Apparel Sourcing Lead, Logistics Procurement Lead, Treasury Working Capital Lead from GlobalRetail Treasury Ltd, AP Operations Lead, GR_APAC_PTE sourcing representative, GlobalRetail US Inc logistics representative  
**Recorder:** Procurement PMO

## Purpose

Review supplier allocation risk for the upcoming seasonal purchase windows before AP and Treasury send notices related to the Net-30 policy rollout / Net-90 policy rollout.

## Terminology used in meeting

The team used **Net-30 policy rollout** to refer to the supplier cohort starting from Net-30 open invoice activity. The team used **Net-90 policy rollout** to refer to the proposed extension or renegotiation target. Both phrases refer to the same payment-term initiative in this meeting.

## Seasonal allocation risk discussion

The Apparel Sourcing Lead noted that the initial working-capital analysis will identify payment-term extension opportunities, but the analysis does not capture recent supplier allocation conversations. The following supplier context was discussed as current and operationally important.

### Jakarta Apparel Mfg 14 - TP_APPAREL_0014

Jakarta Apparel Mfg 14 is providing cut-and-sew capacity for a seasonal apparel program supporting GlobalRetail APAC Pte (`GR_APAC_PTE`) and GlobalRetail Japan KK. The vendor has offered priority allocation during the late Q3 and Q4 buy windows because Procurement verbally confirmed that the vendor would not be pushed into a longer payment cycle during this season.

Procurement stated that the understanding is not in the supplier master, the policy repository, or the standard payment-term documentation. It exists in the sourcing call notes and was referenced again in the CFO monthly discussion.

**Sourcing note:** The vendor has not requested a formal amendment because the buyer team framed the arrangement as seasonal. However, the vendor's allocation desk is treating payment timing as part of the capacity commitment.

**Risk if moved to Net-90:** Loss of allocation priority during the seasonal purchase window, delayed shipment releases, or substitution into lower-priority production slots.

**Recommendation from Procurement:** Exclude Jakarta Apparel Mfg 14 from the first Net-90 supplier notice cycle. Keep Net-30 and early-payment / priority-payment handling available during seasonal windows.

### XPO Logistics Region 7 - TP_LOGI_0007

XPO Logistics Region 7 is being used for seasonal outbound capacity and regional surge coverage supporting GlobalRetail US Inc holiday distribution lanes. Logistics Procurement stated that XPO has not signed a formal capacity amendment. However, the regional account team has repeatedly tied preferred capacity handling to invoice timeliness and dispute resolution speed.

The Logistics Procurement Lead stated that moving XPO Logistics Region 7 into the first Net-90 notice cycle may be interpreted by the carrier as a change in commercial posture before the seasonal routing plan is locked.

**Risk if moved to Net-90:** Reduced preferred capacity during peak retail shipping periods, slower exception handling on disputed freight invoices, or re-pricing of surge lanes.

**Recommendation from Procurement:** Defer supplier notice until the logistics capacity lead confirms that the Net-90 rollout will not impair seasonal coverage.
