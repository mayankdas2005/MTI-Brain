# CFO Finance / Treasury Monthly Discussion - Supplier Payment-Term Review

## Group and entity context

These notes relate to **GlobalRetail Holdings plc** (`GR_HOLDINGS`, LEI `LEI-GRHOLD`), the UK parent company of a 25-entity GlobalRetail group. The parent functional currency is **GBP**. Working-capital analysis, supplier-notice decisions, and CFO briefing values in this folder should be treated as group-level analysis for `GR_HOLDINGS` unless a source line identifies a specific regional or operating-company dependency.

**GlobalRetail Treasury Ltd** (`GR_TREASURY`, GBP) is the in-house treasury vehicle coordinating the Net-30 / Net-90 supplier payment-term workstream with the CFO Office, AP Operations, Procurement, Sourcing, and Store Development Finance. Regional context may involve **GlobalRetail Europe BV** (`GR_EU_BV`), **GlobalRetail APAC Pte** (`GR_APAC_PTE`), **GlobalRetail LATAM SA** (`GR_LATAM_SA`), and country operating companies such as GlobalRetail US Inc, GlobalRetail UK Ltd, GlobalRetail Deutschland GmbH, and GlobalRetail Japan KK.

## Entity scope noted during the meeting

The CFO confirmed that the supplier-payment review is a `GR_HOLDINGS` group working-capital initiative coordinated through `GR_TREASURY`. The AP extract includes invoices booked across country operating companies, but the CFO-ready benefit view should be consolidated in GBP. Regional sub-holdings should be consulted where supplier allocation or service continuity is region-specific.


**Date:** 2026-05-29  
**Meeting type:** Monthly CFO Finance / Treasury review  
**Attendees:** CFO of GlobalRetail Holdings plc, Group Treasurer of GlobalRetail Treasury Ltd, VP Procurement, AP Director, Sourcing Lead, Store Development Finance Lead, regional finance leads from GR_EU_BV, GR_APAC_PTE, and GR_LATAM_SA  
**Recorder:** Treasury PMO, GlobalRetail Treasury Ltd (`GR_TREASURY`)

## Agenda items

1. Working-capital opportunity from moving selected Net-30 suppliers to Net-90.
2. Supplier-notice timing and exposure to peak-season purchasing windows.
3. Exception handling before AP issues notices.
4. CFO-office decisions that have not yet been reflected in policy or payment-term documentation.

## Discussion notes

The Group Treasurer of GlobalRetail Treasury Ltd summarized the current payment-term workstream as the **Net-30 policy rollout**. Procurement referred to the same workstream as the **Net-90 policy rollout** because the target state is to renegotiate selected Net-30 open invoices to Net-90. The CFO asked that both labels remain in the working materials until supplier notices are finalized because teams are already using both phrases in meetings.

AP Operations stated that the supplier master extract shows a broad population of suppliers with Net-30 activity. AP can size the initial working-capital benefit from structured invoice data, but AP does not maintain an authoritative list of informal commercial commitments that may require exception handling.

The VP Procurement raised three supplier-specific issues before any supplier notices are sent.

## Supplier-specific discussion

### Jakarta Apparel Mfg 14 - TP_APPAREL_0014 - internal shorthand: Vendor X

Procurement confirmed that **Jakarta Apparel Mfg 14** is formally coded with Net-30 activity in the AP vendor master. The vendor is not currently flagged in the policy or payment-term documentation as protected.

The VP Procurement stated that the vendor received a **verbal sourcing commitment** in the latest seasonal allocation discussion. The commitment is not yet updated in contract, policy, or payment-term documentation. The commitment was made to preserve allocation priority for late Q3 and Q4 seasonal purchase windows supporting GlobalRetail APAC Pte (`GR_APAC_PTE`) and GlobalRetail Japan KK demand plans.

The CFO confirmed the following operating instruction:

> Keep Jakarta Apparel Mfg 14 on Net-30 for the seasonal purchase window. Do not include them in the first Net-90 supplier notice cycle until Procurement confirms that allocation priority is no longer dependent on payment timing.

The CFO also noted that Procurement had received an update that Jakarta Apparel Mfg 14 has been granted an operational exception by the CFO Office for early payment or priority payment during seasonal purchase windows. The exception is not intended to create a permanent payment-term policy change, but it is binding for the upcoming seasonal purchase cycle.

### XPO Logistics Region 7 - TP_LOGI_0007 - internal shorthand: Vendor Y

The logistics team uses XPO Logistics Region 7 for high-volume seasonal outbound coverage and surge handling, primarily supporting GlobalRetail US Inc distribution lanes during the holiday surge. Procurement noted that XPO's capacity desk has informally tied preferred linehaul capacity to payment reliability and invoice dispute resolution speed.

The CFO did not approve a permanent exclusion from the Net-90 policy rollout. The CFO asked Treasury to **defer the notice** until Procurement confirms whether the Net-90 change would affect holiday capacity allocation.

### CAPEX Vendor 1 - TP_CAPEX_0001 - internal shorthand: Vendor Z

Store Development Finance noted that CAPEX Vendor 1 is a high-impact supplier in the Net-30 population and will appear attractive in any cash-flow impact ranking, especially for store-readiness workstreams led through GlobalRetail Europe BV (`GR_EU_BV`), GlobalRetail UK Ltd, and GlobalRetail Deutschland GmbH. However, the vendor is tied to several store-opening workstreams and pending lien-waiver sequencing.

The CFO asked that any payment-term notice to CAPEX Vendor 1 be routed for CFO / Store Development Finance approval before release. The CFO did not want the working-capital workstream to trigger a store-readiness delay.

## Notes for CFO briefing preparation

The CFO emphasized that the formal AP answer is useful but not sufficient for supplier notices across the GlobalRetail group. The CFO wants the working-capital benefit stated in two ways:

1. **Gross benefit:** based on all suppliers that appear eligible using structured AP data.
2. **Exception-adjusted benefit:** after removing or deferring suppliers with documented meeting-note commitments, operational payment exceptions, or unresolved commercial dependencies.

The CFO asked the Treasurer to make clear that the exception list is not a policy override. It is a risk-control step before the supplier notices are sent.
